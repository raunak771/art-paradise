Getting Started

How to Join

  1. Open Minecraft on your device.
  2. Tap Play on the home screen.
  3. Navigate to the Servers tab.
  4. Scroll down and tap Add Server.
  5. Server name: Art Paradise
  6. Address: mc.artparadise.uk
  7. Port: 19132
  8. Tap Save then select the server.
